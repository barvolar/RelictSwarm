# Relict Swarm audio rights audit (2026-11-03)

## Release decision: NOT CLEARED

**Do not label the embedded soundtrack or sound effects "royalty free", "CC0", or commercially licensed yet.** The repository bundles audio as base64 in `src/modules/30-audio/embedded-bank.js` (approximately 3.5 MB), but it has no asset-by-asset provenance records, original source URLs, licenses, author credits or download dates. A filename and an audio recording are not evidence of licensing terms.

## What was checked

- Repository tree, README, `assets/`, `src/modules/30-audio/`, and published release documentation: no LICENSE, attribution inventory or sound provenance ledger was found.
- Original in-code SVG/vector art and robot skins are generated by the game's source. No externally hosted sprite or font is required by the GitHub Pages build. This is a source-code observation, **not** an independent legal ownership certification.
- Sound effects used by the application include at least: laser, laserHit, arc, rail, railCharge, rocket, plasma, cryo, flame, flame_loop, gravity, gravityCollapse, mine, boomer, boomerReturn, saw, sawHit, explosion, heavyExplosion, heavyExplosionDeep, hit, kill, damage, fusion, bossDown, warning and ui. Music includes menu_theme and combat_theme. The final set of embedded bank keys must be verified from the original sound source manifest.
- One uploaded asset was previously described as `freesound_community-spawn-sound-43782 (mp3cut.net).mp3`, used for mine placement. This filename alone does **not** establish its exact author, Freesound listing or license.

## Blocking evidence to obtain before CrazyGames submission

For **each** non-procedural track or sample, retain:
1. Source page URL and actual creator/uploader.
2. Exact license name and version **as of download**, plus a screenshot or downloaded license text.
3. Whether commercial use, redistribution bundled in a game, and modifications/shortening are allowed.
4. Attribution wording, link and required change notice (when applicable).
5. Whether the recording contains samples from other sources with additional restrictions.

## License rules

Freesound's official FAQ explains that sound uploads can use **CC0**, **CC BY** or **CC BY-NC** (and some historical licenses). CC0 generally permits commercial use; CC BY can permit it with proper attribution; CC BY-NC is **not** suitable for the intended advertising-supported commercial release. Each asset's license must be checked individually, not inferred from the site's name.

Source: https://freesound.org/help/faq/ (Licenses)

## Remediation options

- If the original source and a compatible license can be documented, add an attribution table and an in-game credits entry for each CC BY sound.
- If provenance cannot be established or the license bars commercial use, replace that sound with a properly documented CC0 or self-created alternative. Preserve gameplay timing and player volume controls.
- Do not assume a platform's Basic Launch (without ads) eliminates all copyright and distribution requirements.
