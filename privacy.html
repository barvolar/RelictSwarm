Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains how the independently developed browser game Relict Swarm stores game progress and, only if you choose to allow it, sends gameplay and performance statistics to the developer.

At a glance: You can play without creating an account or allowing optional analytics. Game progress is saved locally in your browser. Refusing analytics does not block gameplay.

1. Who operates this game

Relict Swarm is published by its independent developer, barvolar. This policy covers the game's own data handling, not the separate policies of websites or apps that host the game.

2. Game progress and browser storage

The game uses your browser's localStorage to remember progress and settings, including earned crystals, unlocks, upgrades, skins, achievements recorded in saved game data, audio and graphics preferences, and your analytics choice. If optional telemetry is enabled, a small queue of unsent game reports may also be stored locally.

These game saves remain on your device/browser. They are not automatically synchronized between browsers, devices, or CrazyGames accounts by Relict Swarm. Clearing site data or using another browser may erase or separate your progress.

Relict Swarm does not require registration and does not ask for your real name, email address, password, or payment details.

3. Optional gameplay and performance analytics

If you actively select Allow for optional analytics, the game may transmit technical game reports to a developer-operated Cloudflare Worker and Cloudflare D1 database. These may include:

  • Run outcome and duration, difficulty, level, kills, score and gameplay progression.
  • Weapon choices, upgrades, fusions, boss encounters, and similar in-game events.
  • Performance measurements such as FPS, frame times and rendering workloads.
  • Coarse device category (phone, tablet or desktop), approximate screen-width bucket, and graphics quality.
  • Random per-run session and event identifiers used to group and deduplicate reports. These are not an account ID.

These reports are used to investigate bugs, optimize performance and improve game balance. The game does not intentionally include names, email addresses, exact device identifiers, or raw browser user-agent strings in its analytics reports. Network providers may nonetheless process technical connection information, such as IP addresses, to deliver or secure requests. The game's own telemetry database does not store the request IP address or raw User-Agent.

Game reports are sent only after an affirmative analytics choice, and delivery may depend on the browser and hosting platform's network restrictions. The legal basis for this optional processing, where applicable, is your consent.

4. Your choices and deletion

  • Choose Do not send on the analytics prompt to refuse optional reporting.
  • At any time, open Settings → Optional gameplay analytics → Disable to stop future reports. Disabling also removes queued, unsent reports kept by the game in your browser.
  • Clear Relict Swarm site data in your browser to remove locally stored progress, settings and analytics preferences. This cannot be undone through the game.
  • Disabling analytics or clearing your browser does not automatically erase reports already delivered to the developer's database.

Previously received reports are associated with random per-run identifiers, not player accounts. The developer may be unable to reliably associate a given report with an individual player. For questions or a deletion request, use the contact route below; please do not post personal information publicly.

5. Storage, retention and security

Optional analytics reports are processed using Cloudflare Workers and stored in a developer-managed Cloudflare D1 database. Access to detailed reports is restricted to developer administration. The game does not sell optional analytics reports or use them for targeted advertising.

There is currently no automatic expiry schedule configured for reports already stored in the analytics database; they remain until the developer deletes them as part of maintenance or in response to a valid request where the records can be identified. Browser-stored data remains until you or the browser remove it.

Cloudflare may process technical request data under its own operational policies. Although access to the developer database is restricted, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Hosting platforms and other services

When you play on CrazyGames, the CrazyGames platform and SDK may process separate platform data (such as game-loading and gameplay-start events) under CrazyGames' Privacy Policy. These events are separate from the developer's optional telemetry described above.

The game can also be hosted on GitHub Pages. Browser/hosting providers may process essential connection and security logs under their own policies. The game does not integrate a separate advertising provider or third-party login.

7. Children and changes

The game is intended for the general gaming audience of the platforms where it is made available. It does not knowingly request a child's name, email or account details. If our data practices change, this page will be updated with a revised date.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, access or deletion requests, contact the developer through Relict Swarm's GitHub issue tracker. Issues are publicly visible, so do not include private or sensitive information. If a private channel is necessary, ask the developer to arrange one before sharing details.
