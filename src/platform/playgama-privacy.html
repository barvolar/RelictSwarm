Relict Swarm: Privacy Information

Relict Swarm runs as an HTML5 game on Playgama and participating game portals. This Playgama edition does not send our own gameplay telemetry to Cloudflare or other developer-operated analytics servers.

Game progress, unlocked skins, settings and scores are saved through Playgama Bridge Storage. Storage may be local or cloud-based depending on the host platform.

Ads are delivered through Playgama Bridge and may involve the host platform's advertising partners. The host platform's privacy terms explain how advertising data is processed.

The game does not require you to register a separate account. Please consult the host platform's privacy policy for its account, SDK, ad and cloud-storage practices.
